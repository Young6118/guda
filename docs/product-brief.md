# Product Brief

## Vision

Build a global user demand intelligence platform that continuously monitors public and authorized discussion channels, extracts real user pain points, and turns fragmented community signals into evidence-backed opportunity analysis.

The platform should cover broad human needs and market categories, not only AI. AI is used to collect, clean, cluster, reason over, and explain the data. It is also one possible class of solutions surfaced by the platform.

## Core Insight

Many high-value user signals live inside separated traffic pools:

- Developer communities: GitHub, V2EX, Hacker News, Stack Overflow, Reddit, X.
- High-knowledge communities: Zhihu, WeChat public accounts, newsletters, research blogs.
- Consumer communities: Xiaohongshu, Douyin, Bilibili, TikTok, YouTube, Instagram.
- Market and finance communities: X, Reddit, Xueqiu, Seeking Alpha, financial news, filings.
- Industry channels: semiconductor news, supply chain reports, job posts, vendor forums, procurement discussions.

These pools are hard to search uniformly. The product value comes from normalizing them into one evidence system.

## Jobs To Be Done

1. As a founder, I want to discover repeated pain points before building a product.
2. As a product manager, I want to know which features users keep asking for across communities.
3. As an investor, I want to detect categories whose discussion volume and urgency are rising.
4. As a marketer, I want to understand how audiences describe their problems in their own words.
5. As an operator, I want to track hot directions and get alerting when a topic spikes.

## First-Class Product Objects

### Source

A configured data source, such as a platform, API endpoint, forum board, subreddit, GitHub query, RSS feed, paid data provider, or manually curated URL list.

### Topic Pack

A managed research theme, such as AI agents, video creation, ecommerce sourcing, semiconductor, financial markets, creator tools, marketing automation, or education tools.

### Collection Task

A scheduled task that fetches data from one or more sources according to a query, cadence, and budget.

### Evidence Item

A normalized post, comment, issue, article, review, video comment, transcript snippet, or other atomic unit of user expression.

### Insight

An AI/human-reviewed finding backed by evidence items. Examples: pain point, emerging trend, unmet need, competitor gap, feature request, willingness-to-pay signal.

### Opportunity

A higher-level synthesis combining insights, audience, use case, urgency, feasibility, market evidence, and possible solution directions.

## MVP Outcome

The MVP should prove that the system can:

1. Configure several heterogeneous sources.
2. Run recurring collection tasks.
3. Store normalized evidence.
4. Deduplicate and cluster discussion items.
5. Produce AI-assisted pain point and trend summaries with citations.
6. Show a dashboard of topic volume, rising terms, representative cases, and opportunity cards.
